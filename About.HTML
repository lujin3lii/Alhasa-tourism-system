About Us

Al-Ahsa Vision Hub is an innovative smart tourism platform designed to enhance the travel experience in Al-Ahsa. The platform acts as a virtual personal tour guide, helping tourists discover attractions, events, restaurants, and local experiences in one place. By using artificial intelligence, the system analyzes user preferences and behavior to generate personalized travel itineraries based on interests and length of stay. Our platform also aims to support local artisans, small businesses, and young entrepreneurs by connecting them directly with visitors, contributing to sustainable economic growth and promoting Al-Ahsa as a leading tourist destination.
